    Glossary

    What the icons and colors mean, and how the linking system works.

    Connectors

    🔗
    Bridge — this number appears on two or more warps. Both ends are confirmed connected. Click it to jump straight to the other end.
    ⊕
    Pending — only one warp has this number so far. It's a placeholder waiting for its match.
    ×
    Next to a badge: unlinks just that warp from the connector. In the Connectors index: deletes the connector everywhere (click twice to confirm).

    Floor & door

    Two optional fields for buildings with internal structure — a floor (e.g. 1F, 2F) and a door #, each shown as a small badge on the row. Leave either blank for a warp they don't apply to.

    Row actions

    🔗
    Link — click on one warp, then click it on another to connect them. Reuses a number if either side already has one, otherwise hands out the next free number.
    ✎
    Edit — opens the entry point, destination and connector fields for that warp. Click the checkmark to lock it back in.
    ×
    Delete — removes a warp. On a whole location, needs a second click to confirm.
    ☐
    Checkbox — mark a warp as logged once you know where it leads.

    Tags

    Notable
    Boss fight, legendary, or another important encounter.
    Warning
    Risky, one-way, or otherwise dangerous to take blind.
    Useless
    Dead end — nothing worth backtracking for.
    Unconfirmed
    Noted, but not personally verified yet.
